# frgmt.xyz — route 04, Watermark

Previous route (03 Vitrine) archived at BRIEF-vitrine.md. This is a restart, not a revision.

**What changed** Beckett is retired and Kowo depended on it. There is no flagship project.
The honest state is: reading, resting, small experiments, school, the ambassador role, and
ideas that have not landed yet. The one thing on the site that is alive is the writing.

**What it is** A writing-first personal site. Three rooms: Home, Essays, Notes. The name
finally means something: frgmt, fragments — short dated notes that are not essays yet.
Projects get an invisible placeholder: the room exists in the information architecture but
renders nothing until there is something real to put in it. Beckett and Kowo appear nowhere
on the homepage; the essays about them still exist and are not edited.

**The opening** First person, now-first, no credentials:

> I'm Jason. Right now I'm mostly reading, resting, and circling ideas that haven't
> landed yet. This site is where they land first.

**Voice** The blog voice, everywhere. First person, concrete, plain, parenthetical asides,
honest numbers, no persuasion. No em-dashes on the site. Nothing that reads like marketing.

**Visual route: painted, but restrained.** Warm paper and ink, and pigment enters in exactly
one way: watercolor rendered with p5.brush, in the spirit of Steven Holl's 5x7 concept
studies — quick washes, reserved paper, one pigment at a time, a few broken ink lines.
Reference: stevenholl.com watercolors; p5.brush by Alejandro Campos (p5-brush.cargo.site).

Where paint appears:
- One wash on Home, behind or beside the opening. Mostly water. The quietest possible hero.
- Small pigment blooms as date stamps beside essays and notes, one bloom per entry, seeded
  by the entry so it is stable across visits.
- A ragged wash strip as the only divider anywhere.
- Dark mode is the painting: a fixed full-viewport nocturne (moonlit sky, clouds, a grassy
  knoll, voluminous trees flanking the margins) sits behind every page, and content floats
  in the night air. The divider turns moss green and the blooms keep their chroma. Light
  stays quiet paper; turning the lamp off steps into the landscape.
- Assets bake on pure white (light) and pure black (dark) and reach the page through
  mix-blend-mode multiply and screen respectively, so pigment alone survives the blend and
  can sit on paper or on the painted backdrop without crop-rectangle seams.

Paint ships as pre-rendered images (WebP), generated by design/tools/paint. p5 is ~1MB and
does not belong in the bundle. A live seeded-painting mode is a future idea, not this route.

Pigments, one per room, all near-monochrome against `#FAFAF7` paper and `#17171B` ink:
- payne's gray `#46536b` — Home
- indigo `#2c3a68` — Essays
- raw umber `#6e5237` — Notes

**Structure**
- `/` Home: opening paragraph, a short Now, five most recent pieces of writing (essays and
  notes interleaved, dated), email, elsewhere links.
- `/essays` every finished piece, one dated line each. The current blog, renamed.
- `/notes` the fragments stream: dated, titles optional, a paragraph or less each,
  low ceremony. Written from the same admin.
- `/admin` carries over. Worker API and existing posts carry over. Notes are a new content
  type in the same store.
- Projects: reserved id in nav order, renders nothing while empty.

**Keep** Existing posts and admin, dark mode, 390/768/1440, WCAG AA, visible focus,
reduced motion honoured, no analytics. Footer stays copyright-only.

**Contact** jason@frgmt.xyz printed on Home. Needs Cloudflare Email Routing enabled to
forward to the real inbox; site should not ship the address until routing works.

**Deliberately out**
- Beckett, Kowo, the old three-object Work section, the ascii truchet wash, the dither
  plate. Route 03 is over.
- Handwriting fonts. Warmth comes from paint and voice, not costume.
- Any project section content until a real project exists.
